## Brand & Style

The brand personality is **Pragmatic, Local, and Trustworthy**. It avoids "concept-stage" flourishes like heavy gradients or glassmorphism, focusing instead on a high-density, utility-first experience reminiscent of market-leading food delivery platforms. The design is "real-world" and functional, prioritizing the conversion funnel (discovery to payment) over decorative elements.

The visual style is **Corporate / Modern** with a focus on **Flat UI** principles. Depth is communicated through background color shifts and hairline borders rather than shadows. The goal is to evoke a sense of efficiency and reliability for campus users and merchants alike.

## Colors

The palette is centered on a high-energy, warm brand color to stimulate appetite and signal action.

- **Primary**: A warm orange-red used for CTAs, price highlights, and active states.
- **Secondary**: A deep charcoal for high-contrast typography and headers.
- **Surface**: The design utilizes a layered approach where the page background is a subtle gray (`#F7F7F7`) and individual cards or content modules are pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Functional**: Muted grays are strictly utilized for secondary descriptions and auxiliary data to maintain clear information hierarchy.

## Typography

This design system uses a compact typographic scale optimized for mobile density. 

- **Hierarchy**: Use `headline-lg` for page headers and `headline-md` for merchant names. `body-lg` is reserved for product titles.
- **Clarity**: Secondary information (distance, delivery fees, "month sold" stats) must use `label-md` or `label-sm` with the tertiary text color to de-emphasize them relative to prices and titles.
- **Weight**: Semibold and Bold weights are used strategically for prices and primary actions to ensure they stand out in a high-density layout.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** philosophy tailored for mobile viewports, emphasizing high information density.

- **Rhythm**: A 4px base unit governs all spacing. Page margins are set to 12px to maximize horizontal space for product listings.
- **Structure**: Use a "Left-Category / Right-Product" vertical split for merchant menus. This two-column approach allows for rapid navigation.
- **Breakpoints**: 
  - **Mobile (<480px)**: Single column of cards or the category-product split.
  - **Tablet (481px - 768px)**: Cards transition to a 2-column grid to prevent excessive line lengths.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system explicitly rejects ambient shadows. Depth is achieved through **Tonal Layers** and **Low-contrast outlines**.

- **Z-Index Logic**:
  - **Level 0 (Base)**: The `#F7F7F7` page background.
  - **Level 1 (Content)**: White cards with a 1px `#E5E5E5` border.
  - **Level 2 (Navigation)**: Sticky headers and bottom tab bars, using a subtle border-top or border-bottom for separation.
  - **Level 3 (Overlays)**: Bottom-sheet drawers for product specifications or filters. These use a dimming backdrop (40% black) to focus the user.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft** and restrained. Large, expressive pill shapes are avoided to maintain a professional, utility-centric aesthetic.

- **Standard Radius**: 4px for smaller elements like input fields and tags.
- **Container Radius**: 8px for merchant cards and product images.
- **Prohibited**: Do not use fully rounded (pill) buttons; maintain the 4px-8px logic to ensure the UI feels "constructed" and precise.

## Components

- **Buttons**: Primary buttons use the brand orange background with white text. They are rectangular with an 8px corner radius. No shadows are applied.
- **Cards**: Cards are white containers on the light gray background. They use a 1px hairline border (`#E5E5E5`) for definition. Internal padding is strictly 10px-12px.
- **Chips/Tags**: Small tags (e.g., "Discount", "New") use a 4px radius and either a light brand tint background or a simple border.
- **Input Fields**: Ghost-style inputs with a 1px border. No inner shadows.
- **Bottom Drawers**: Used for selecting food specs. They slide up from the bottom, occupying 60-75% of the screen height, featuring a clear "Close" or "Confirm" action at the base.
- **Lists**: List items within a card are separated by a 1px horizontal divider (`#E5E5E5`), stopping 12px short of the card edge for a clean finish.
